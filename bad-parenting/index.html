About Bad Parenting

Bad Parenting is a psychological horror game that has taken the internet by storm. This haunting narrative-driven experience explores the dark side of family relationships through disturbing storytelling and atmospheric horror elements.

Step into a twisted household where nothing is as it seems. Through point-and-click gameplay, you'll uncover disturbing family secrets, witness psychological manipulation, and experience a story that will leave you questioning everything you thought you knew about parenting and family dynamics.

Game Features:

  • Psychological horror storytelling with mature themes
  • Atmospheric sound design and eerie visuals
  • Point-and-click adventure gameplay
  • Multiple story paths and endings
  • Deep narrative exploring family dysfunction
  • Viral internet sensation with widespread discussion
  • Short but impactful gaming experience

Content Warning: This game contains mature themes including psychological abuse, family dysfunction, and disturbing imagery. Player discretion is advised. The game deals with sensitive topics that may not be suitable for all audiences.

How to Play Bad Parenting

Use your mouse to interact with objects and navigate through the story. Click on highlighted items to examine them, interact with characters through dialogue choices, and piece together the disturbing narrative. Pay attention to environmental details and subtle clues that reveal the true nature of the family dynamics. Some choices may affect the story outcome, so think carefully about your decisions.
